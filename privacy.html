Privacy Policy

Digital Privacy Assistant
Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how Digital Privacy Assistant handles information when you use the website. The service helps you find online accounts using messages in a Gmail inbox you choose to connect.

Information we access

If you choose to connect Google, the website requests read-only Gmail access. It checks email sender and subject information to identify account-related messages. It does not read email message bodies or send, change, or delete Gmail messages. You can disconnect or revoke access through your Google Account settings.

Information processed

The service processes your Google account email address and matching message details to show possible accounts and scan results. It may check the email address against third-party breach information services to report known exposure results.

Storage and sharing

Scan information may be sent to and processed by the website's hosting provider to provide the service. We do not sell personal information. We do not share Gmail message contents because the service does not access message bodies. The website may use third-party providers for hosting and breach checks, which process information only to provide their services.

Retention and deletion

You can stop further Gmail access by disconnecting the account or revoking access in your Google Account. Information already processed or saved by the service may remain until deleted by the site operator or its hosting/database provider. For a deletion request, contact the project owner using the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not use the service if you are not comfortable granting read-only access to the selected Gmail account.

Children

This service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the service changes. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the project owner using the support email listed on the Google sign-in consent screen.
