Digital Privacy Assistant

Find forgotten accounts, check which ones are risky, and delete the ones you no longer use.

The problem

People sign up on many websites and apps and then forget them. Those accounts still hold personal details such as name, phone number and address, and these details can leak in a data breach.

What the app does

  • Finds accounts linked to your email address, using only the sender and subject of signup, verification, password-reset and order emails in your Gmail (read-only).
  • Shows which accounts appear in known data breaches and what kind of data leaked.
  • Ranks which accounts to clean up first and gives a simple privacy score.
  • Helps you delete unused accounts with steps, a ready-made request letter, and follow-up reminders.
  • Checks whether a password has appeared in known breaches, without sending the password anywhere.

Your data

The app never reads the body of your emails and never changes or deletes anything in your Gmail. Read the full Privacy Policy.
